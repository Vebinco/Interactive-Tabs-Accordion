Frequently Asked Questions

What is the return policy? +

Our return policy allows for returns within 30 days of purchase. The item must be in its original condition with all tags and packaging intact. Please visit our returns page for more detailed instructions.

How do I track my order? +

Once your order has shipped, you will receive an email with a tracking number and a link to the carrier's website. You can use this information to track the status of your delivery.

Can I change my shipping address? +

If your order has not yet been shipped, you can contact our customer support team to request a change of address. Please have your order number ready when you call.
